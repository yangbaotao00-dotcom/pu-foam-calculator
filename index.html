How this PU foam calculator works

Method and formula

For metric inputs, joint volume in liters = total length (m) × gap width (mm) × gap depth (mm) ÷ 1,000.

Usable yield per can = nominal free-foam yield × usable-yield percentage. Estimated cans = round up [joint volume × (1 + waste allowance) ÷ usable yield per can].

Imperial units are converted to SI internally before calculation. Input the total length of all joints to estimate.

Important limitations

  • Nominal free-foam yield is not a guarantee of installed joint fill volume.
  • Moisture, temperature, substrate, joint geometry, application method, and product formulation may change actual consumption.
  • Do not use this estimate to validate fire resistance, structural performance, or manufacturer certification.
  • Use verified product technical documents and follow site-specific safety guidance.
